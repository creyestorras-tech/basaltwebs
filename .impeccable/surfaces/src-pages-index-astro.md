## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a dressed basalt wall from Olot: each section a course of stone, joints of lime mortar as the only grid, headlines incised. It refuses the SaaS split hero with a feature-card row.

OWN-WORLD: Basalt near-black #131517 with real photographed grain; slate #24282c blocks; lime-mortar #e7e5df courses for reading; mist #8b9196; one lichen accent #e3a12f (Xanthoria on old walls) for actions only. Marcellus (glyphic, stone-cut) display, Schibsted Grotesk text. Joints are 2px gaps showing the darker bed beneath; running-bond offsets; no gradients, no glows, no rounded pill soup.

STORY: Understand the product (book on the phone, see it land on the panel) in ten seconds; believe it (three labelled example sites, each in its own style); feel zero risk (free proposal, one payment, no fees); write on WhatsApp.
